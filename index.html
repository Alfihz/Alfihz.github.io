About me

Engineer by wiring,
tinkerer by nature.

I started my journey studying Electrical Engineering across Indonesia and Germany, picking up everything from PID control in oil refineries to IoT systems on Caterpillar engines. Along the way, I realised I was most alive when the systems I built could think.

That curiosity pulled me into AI — first through quantization research on large neural networks at Aptiv's AI-Architecture Team, then through hands-on projects in anomaly detection, RAG chatbots, and reinforcement learning. I'm currently pursuing a Master's in Computer Engineering at RWTH Aachen while building toward a full transition into AI/ML Engineering.

Beyond code, I chair TechLabs Aachen — organising hackathons, Python workshops, and industry collaborations. Teaching others is how I solidify what I know.
